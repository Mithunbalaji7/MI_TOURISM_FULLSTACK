🌦 Weather Information

☀ Summer

Temperature ranges from 30°C to 40°C. Carry light cotton clothes, water bottles and sunscreen.

🌧 Monsoon

Pleasant climate with moderate rainfall. Carry umbrellas, raincoats and waterproof footwear.

❄ Winter

Cool and comfortable weather between 18°C and 28°C. Best season for sightseeing.

💡 Essential Travel Tips

📄 Carry Documents

Always keep valid ID proof, tickets and hotel confirmations.

💳 Digital Payments

Carry some cash, but UPI and cards are accepted almost everywhere.

🎒 Pack Smart

Bring comfortable shoes, medicines, power bank and water bottle.

📍 Stay Safe

Follow local guidelines, keep valuables secure, and save emergency numbers.
