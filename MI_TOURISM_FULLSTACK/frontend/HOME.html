ABOUT US

Vanakkam!
Step into the warmth of Tamil Nadu, where tradition meets modernity and hospitality knows no bounds! MI TOURISM DEVELOPMENT is a true marvel of the tourism industry. Founded in 2011 with a humble beginning, MITD has now become a giant in the hospitality sector. With over 53 hotels, youth hostels, aalayams, tour packages (one-day tours and multiple-day tours), eight boat houses, three telescope houses, a chain of restaurants, and more, MITD has succeeded in showcasing the wonders of Tamil Nadu to the world. The organization’s extensive offering of services reflects its dedication to quality. With a focus on promoting tourism, MITD has created a seamless and unforgettable experience for every visitor. Come experience the enchanting beauty of Tamil Nadu with MITD..
